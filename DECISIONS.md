# Decision log

One entry per decision: date, decision, reason.

- Code lives in files and syncs to Studio with Rojo. Studio is a viewer only.
- 2026-10-30: The map is built by `src/server/MapBuilder.luau` at server start from `Config/World` (same seed every time), so the world lives in code like everything else.
- 2026-10-30: Vehicles pass through players, NPCs, debris and each other (collision groups). NPC hits, building breaks and player hits are found by the driver's client with an overlap box and checked on the server.
- 2026-10-30: Vehicle meshes are built in Blender (`tools/blender/export_vehicles.py`), uploaded once, downloaded back and packed into `assets/Vehicles.rbxm` (`tools/lune/import_vehicles.luau`); rigs are built in code at spawn (`VehicleBuilder`).
- 2026-10-30: Game pass ids are 0 until the passes exist; in Studio the store toggles them for testing.
- 2026-10-30: First session: spawn a car (garage opens and glows) → run over 3 people (a crowd of 12 always waits at the spawn plaza, an arrow marks the nearest one) → $250 bonus and confetti → "Next ride" savings bar toward the cheapest vehicle you don't own; every purchase gets a confetti moment. Step saved in the profile (`tutorial`); saves from before it skip it.
- 2026-10-30: "Run over 3 people" is the tutorial goal (TUTORIAL pill). After it, hourly and daily goals (3 each, per player, seeded by user id and period so a rejoin shows the same ones, paid on completion, not counted toward "Earn") show in a GOALS panel on the right. The building goal is only offered to owners of a building-breaking vehicle.
- 2026-10-30: One UI style for every screen (`src/client/Style.luau`): dark glass panels with a light edge, Fredoka One headlines, Builder Sans text, gradient buttons that squash when pressed. A UIGradient tints a button's text too, so button colour lives in BackgroundColor3 and the gradient only shades it; no gradients on ViewportFrames (they tint the model).
- 2026-10-30: Game feel lives in `src/client/Fx.luau`, driven by one server→client message (`Fx`: Hit, Land, Break, Boom). Your own hits shake the camera (trauma model), flash the screen and punch the FOV; combos announce DOUBLE!/TRIPLE!/…/RAMPAGE!; landings show "N STUDS!" (HOME RUN at 150+). Sounds are pooled and made once so they play on time. No true slow-motion (Roblox physics can't be slowed per player).
- 2026-10-30: Leaderboards are weekly (reset Monday 00:00 UTC) on three boards at the spawn plaza: most run over, biggest launch, biggest combo. Scores go to one OrderedDataStore per board per week (`Lb_<key>_w<week>`), read once a minute and mixed with the live numbers of the players in the server; all-time numbers are kept in the profile for later. The player list shows Money and all-time "Run Over".
- 2026-10-30: Shop: four Robux money packs ($5k R$25, $25k R$99, $120k R$399, $600k R$1,499; better value as they grow), a once-per-account Starter Pack ($10,000 + Taxi, R$49) offered once after the tutorial's first rampage, and a "NEED $X MORE" offer when you tap BUY on a car you can't afford (suggests the smallest pack that covers the gap; "KEEP DRIVING" closes it). No fake timers. Receipts are granted once (PurchaseId kept in the profile) and confirmed only after the profile saves. Product ids are 0 until created; Studio grants them for testing.
- 2026-10-30: Server events, one every 6 min for 2 min (first after 90 s), announced 20 s ahead in a banner: Low Gravity (gravity x0.35), Golden Rush (8 golden pedestrians near players pay 10x), Giant in Town (a 5x friend NPC downtown; 5 hits, $1,500 each, $5,000 for the topple), Stampede (25 extra pedestrians, 2x pay). The order rotates from a random start per server.
- 2026-10-30: Launch prep: Roblox analytics (onboarding funnel Joined → Spawned a car → First hit → Tutorial done → First vehicle bought; economy sources/sinks with play earnings batched per minute; custom events for spawns, self-destructs (since removed), giant topples). Profiles carry a session lock ({ job, at }, stale after 150 s) taken with UpdateAsync, so a server waits for another to let go before loading. Every remote is validated and rate limited (`RateLimit.luau`).
- 2026-11-09: Rookie pay: hits on people pay x2 until a player first holds the Sedan's price in cash or owns the Sedan or anything at least as expensive (Economy.RookieMultiplier / RookieUntil); it then ends for good (saved as `rookieDone`), so spending back down or never buying a car can't keep it. Measured on a fresh profile with a human driver: about $250/min overall, $420/min while hunting, so the $3,000 Sedan took 6-9 min; replaying that run at x2 gives 2-4 min. That was before the longer tutorial (up to $1,000 in rewards); with it, x2 is estimated at about 2-4 min but not yet measured, so lower RookieMultiplier if new players reach the Sedan much faster. Scooter, Cart and Sedan prices stay as set. No next-car bar on the HUD: pinned goals replaced the old next-ride card on purpose.
- 2026-11-09: The tutorial runs on past the first 3 hits: take a ramp, smash a building (5 pieces), claim a first goal (goals pay by themselves, so the goal step adds one real CLAIM row on top of the sheet; the server only checks the step, like the like reward: $200 once), buy a car, join an event (any hit, smash or landed jump while one is live). Things done early pass their step and still pay; every new step has a SKIP after 10 s (no reward); the event step ends itself after 45 s when no event is live or about to start. Old saves on the retired step 3 count as finished. Goals tab shows from the goal step; pinned goals, the streak card and the Starter offer wait for the end. Funnel renumbered: 4 First rampage, 5 ramp, 6 smash, 7 goal, 8 First vehicle bought, 9 event, 10 Tutorial done. Markers: a big bobbing billboard, a light column over ramps and buildings, an edge-of-screen pointer, and an arrow at the button to press.
- 2026-11-10: Hitting other players and their cars is free for everyone; the Wrecker game pass and self-destruct are gone. So being hit never ruins a game (Config/PlayerHits): 3 s spawn shield; after a hit nobody can hit you for 6 s; ragdoll 1.5 s on foot; a flung car drives again after 0.8 s (1.5 s parked); launches capped at 130 studs/s on foot, 160 for cars; the same attacker hitting you again within 60 s of their last hit does half as much each time, never under a quarter. Cars priced $100,000 and up (Bulldozer, Monster Truck, Supercar, Roadster) skip all of that except a 2 s shield, so their owners can bother other players.
